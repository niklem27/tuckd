Creamy Lemon Garlic Pasta
⏱ 20 min🍽 Serves 4★ 4.9 (1.2k)
Ingredients
  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • Zest and juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 cup grated parmesan
  • Fresh basil, salt, pepper
Instructions
  1. Cook pasta until al dente; reserve 1 cup water.
  2. Sauté garlic in butter for 1 minute.
  3. Stir in cream, lemon and parmesan.
